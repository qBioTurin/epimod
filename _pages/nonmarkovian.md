## Non-Markovian Stochastic Modeling

GreatMod extends beyond traditional memoryless (Markovian) processes by supporting **general probability distributions** for event timing. This enables more realistic modeling of biological processes where event rates depend on elapsed time.

### Key Features
- **General distributions**: Simulate events following Weibull, log-normal, gamma, or custom distributions
- **Memory effects**: Capture age-dependent or history-dependent processes
- **Realistic dynamics**: Model phenomena such as:
  - Cell cycle progression with age structure
  - Immune response maturation
  - Time-dependent recovery rates
  - Non-exponential waiting times in biochemical reactions
